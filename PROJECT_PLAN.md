# E-Commerce Delivery Performance Analysis

## Business Problem

An e-commerce marketplace needs to improve its delivery performance and customer experience in regards to late deliveries.

This project will analyze order data to pinpoint factors associated with late deliveries and will build a predictive model that will estimate the likelihood of a delivery being late. 

# Primary Objectives
1. Evaluate overall order delivery performance and identify the frequency and severity of delays.
2. Analyze how seller performance, product categories, and geographic location relate to delivery delays.
3. Examine the relationship between delivery delays and customer satisfaction based on review scores. 
4. Explore how delivery performance varies accross different time periods and seasons.
5. Develop meaningful features from order, product, seller, and geographic data for predictive modeling.
6. Build and evaluate a machine learning model to identify orders at risk of delays.
